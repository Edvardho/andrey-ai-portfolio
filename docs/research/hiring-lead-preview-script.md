# Preview test with hiring leads

Use this only after the protected Preview is live. Invite 3–5 hiring leads independently; do not coach their questions.

1. Ask the participant to inspect the portfolio and ask at least five questions, including two follow-ups.
2. Ask whether every answer helped assess the candidate, and mark any unsupported claim or answer that dodged the question.
3. Ask for one concrete conclusion they can draw and what they would still need to establish at interview.
4. Record answers only with consent in a separate research note. The portfolio itself does not collect their dialogue.

Release condition: no critical factual error and at least two thirds of participants can name a concrete useful conclusion. This is qualitative validation, not a conversion claim.
